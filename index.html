How it works

Headphones in. Trainer on. Every step gets a comment.

1

Pick a trainer

Warm and encouraging, playful, or a full-volume Hokkien scolding. Your call. There's even a duo mode where two of them argue about you.

2

Press start

Set a distance, time or pace target if you like. Lock the phone, put it in your pocket, and go.

3

Get coached, live

Your trainer reacts to what's actually happening: your pace, each kilometre, your splits, and the moment you beat a personal record.

The works

A proper running app underneath the shouting.

Live GPS tracking

Distance, pace, speed and your route on a map. Kilometre splits and your fastest split, every run.

Apple Health sync

Heart rate and active calories read from Apple Health during the run, and your workouts saved to your own history.

Spotify controls

Connect Spotify and control what's playing from the run screen. The trainer talks over the music, not instead of it.

Personal records

Your fastest 1K, 5K and 10K, tracked automatically and called out the moment you beat them. Lifetime stats included.

Targets and auto-pause

Set a distance, time or pace target. Progress call-outs at every milestone. Auto-pause when you stop at the lights.

Friends and shoutouts

Follow friends, see their runs in a feed, and send a shoutout that their trainer reads out mid-run.

Background audio

Lock the phone and keep it in your pocket. The commentary keeps coming.

Treadmill mode

No GPS indoors? Run to a time target and your trainer still keeps you company.

Coach chatter dial

Turn the talking up or down. From the occasional check-in to non-stop commentary.

📱 Keep what you already use

It's the voice in your ear, not a new tracker.

Tekan Buddy runs happily alongside Strava, Apple Watch, Garmin, WHOOP, anything. It saves its own run history too, but your usual tracker stays your usual tracker.
